# 06 — Content & Copy

## Tone of Voice
Confident, warm, editorial — never corporate, never gimmicky-casual. Short sentences. Verbs over adjectives. Speaks to one reader, not "customers" as a category.

## Writing Guidelines
- Headlines: 4–8 words, no exclamation marks, no puns forced for the sake of it.
- Body copy: max 2 short sentences per block on homepage (details live on sub-pages).
- Always name the brand family naturally (GreenChillyz, YellowChillyz, GoldenChillyz) rather than "our brands."

## Section Headlines (examples, not final copy)
- Hero: "Taste, Reimagined." / subhead: one line on the three-brand family.
- Brand Story: "One Family. Three Flavors."
- Food Experience: "Every Dish Tells a Story."
- Games: "Play. Earn. Return."
- Rewards: "Your Coins, Your Rewards."
- Locations: "Find Your Nearest Table."
- Franchise: "Grow With GreenChillyz."

## Buttons / CTAs
Verb-first, max 3 words: "View Menu", "Find Outlet", "Start Earning", "Join Free", "See Offers". Never "Click Here" or "Learn More" alone — always specific.

## Microcopy
- Coin balance label: "X Coins Ready to Redeem" (not "Balance: X").
- Empty rewards state: "No offers yet — play a game to earn your first coins."
- Empty reviews state: "Be the first to share your experience at this outlet."

## Error Messages
Plain-language, no technical detail, always paired with a next action. E.g. "We couldn't load offers right now — try refreshing." Never expose stack traces or provider names (Supabase/Railway etc.) to end users.

## Loading Messages
Short, brand-voiced, never jokey during account/payment-adjacent flows: "Finding your nearest outlet…", "Loading your rewards…".

## Section Titles (label style)
Use `label-caps` typography (uppercase, tracked) for eyebrow labels above headlines, e.g. "OUR STORY", "REWARDS", "LOCATIONS" — always 1–2 words.

## Do / Don't
- Do: write every CTA as an action the user takes, not a feature name.
- Don't: use filler marketing language ("delicious," "amazing," "best-in-class") — show, via imagery/specificity, don't tell.
