# 00 — Project Vision

Ref: `DESIGN.md` (Luxe Gastronomy system) governs all visual decisions referenced below.

## Brand
GreenChillyz Group operates three brands under one platform: **GreenChillyz** (flagship, primary green #006B2A), **YellowChillyz** (energetic sub-brand), **GoldenChillyz** (premium tier, gold accent #D4AF37). The homepage represents the Group, not a single outlet.

## Mission
Turn a restaurant visit into a digital relationship. The site is a storytelling platform first, a utility (menu/locator) second.

## Experience Goals
- Feel like Apple (restraint), Awwwards (craft), Stripe (clarity), Framer (motion) — not a food-delivery template.
- Every scroll reveals one idea at a time; no information dumping.
- Motion communicates meaning (state change, causality), never decoration.

## Target Audience
Urban, mobile-first diners 20–45, quality- and design-conscious, familiar with premium D2C brand sites. Secondary audience: prospective franchisees evaluating brand credibility.

## Emotional Journey (macro arc)
Curiosity (hero) → Trust (brand story) → Desire (food/visual) → Delight (games/rewards) → Confidence (locations/reviews) → Action (CTA: join, find outlet, franchise).

## Homepage Success Metrics
- Scroll depth >75% median session.
- CTA (join loyalty / find outlet) click-through >8%.
- LCP < 2.0s, CLS < 0.05, INP < 200ms (see `08_Performance_Guidelines.md`).
- Bounce rate under industry median for F&B (~40%).

## Creative Direction
Cinematic Organic aesthetic per `DESIGN.md`: glassmorphism over food photography, organic blob shapes, Poppins type, generous 8px-based spacing, pill-shaped controls. Warm neutral base (#FFF8F0), green as the "living" color, gold reserved strictly for premium/signature moments.

## Long-Term Vision
Homepage architecture must absorb future modules (mobile app teaser, POS-linked ordering, franchise portal) without structural rework — see `16_Project_TODO.md` for phased roadmap.
